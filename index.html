01 · One setup for all five moves

Keep the same positions and direction of travel. P2 serves from ahead of P1, and P1 never turns away from goal.

Sequence

  1. P2 serves from ahead.
  2. P1 receives in a forward run.
  3. Execute move at large cone.
  4. Exit forward and finish.

Two-player rotation

  1. P1 shoots and retrieves.
  2. P2 becomes next attacker.
  3. P1 takes server position.
  4. Alternate every shot.
Main coaching cue: Approach under control -> sell the move -> accelerate forward -> open hips to the shooting target -> finish. No turn.

03 · Finishing progression

Build clean finishing first, then connect the skills. Serve from ahead in the player's direction of travel.

01 · FOUNDATION

Simple pass -> finish

P2 passes back from ahead. P1 arrives facing goal and finishes first time or with a controlled second touch.

02 · CONTROL

Receive -> set -> finish

First touch creates the shooting lane. Next action is a composed finish. No turn.

03 · SKILL

Move -> forward exit -> finish

Use one of the five skills around the large cone. Exit toward goal and shoot.

04 · DECISION

Choose the move -> finish

Alternate left and right exits. Add a passive defender only after technique is consistent.

05 · Shooting Technique - Wide Angle Finish

A separate finishing technique block using your new recording. The diagram is a suggested 2-player setup, not a measurement of the filmed positions.

Wide Angle Finish - your uploaded demonstration.

Execution - 2 players

  1. P1 starts in the wide channel facing goal.
  2. P2 serves a ground ball from ahead into P1's forward path.
  3. P1 approaches at an angle, opens the hips to see the far post, and finishes without turning.
  4. P1 collects the shot. Players exchange attacker and server roles.
  5. Repeat from the opposite wide channel to train both sides.

Shooting technique cues

  1. Last preparation step is controlled, not rushed.
  2. Plant foot beside the ball, aimed toward the chosen target.
  3. Open the hips enough to see and strike toward the far side.
  4. Head steady and eyes on the ball at contact.
  5. Prioritize accurate placement before power.
Progression: 5 accurate finishes per side, then 5 each side with a faster approach. Keep the ball moving forward.

06 · Skill-Move Technique Reference

Demonstration: Balance to Ball Transition - body feint. Use the same movement principles for Scissors, La Croqueta, and both combinations.

Original supplied recording - body control into a feint and ball action.

What to look for

  1. Controlled approach: Stay balanced and close enough to sell the move.
  2. Convincing deception: Use the shoulder, hips and weight shift, not just the foot.
  3. Ball stays close: Make the next touch deliberate and controlled.
  4. Explosive exit: Accelerate into space after the move.
  5. Simple finish: Keep moving toward goal, open hips as needed and shoot without turning.
Apply to all five columns: Sell -> shift -> exit forward -> finish. The video demonstrates the feint principle, not every named skill move.

When to Exit - Defender Reaches

Actual reference clip: the attacker draws the defender into a challenge, then escapes into space.

Read the Defender, Then Exit

  1. Approach: Carry the ball under control and close the distance.
  2. Sell: Show one direction with your body to invite a challenge.
  3. Recognize the reach: Watch for the defender extending a foot or shifting weight to win the ball.
  4. Exit immediately: Move the ball away from the reaching foot into the available forward lane.
  5. Accelerate: Take the next two or three steps with purpose, then finish.
Main cue: Make the defender commit -> touch away -> accelerate. Do not wait for contact.

Apply to all five moves: Feint, Scissors, La Croqueta, Lunge + La Croqueta, and Scissors + La Croqueta. The exact foot action changes, but the exit trigger stays the same.

2-player practice: P2 acts as a passive defender near the large cone and reaches toward the ball without tackling. P1 performs the move and exits forward to finish. Alternate roles after each repetition.

07 · Fundamentals for 1v1 Attacking

Read the defender before choosing your exit. The defender's reaction determines your next action.

Remember: If they reach, exit away from the foot. If they commit without reaching, attack their momentum. If they stay balanced, provoke a shift, change angle, or retain possession.
